About

From the circuit board to the ground station.

Based in Boston Originally from Italy, with stops in Switzerland and at CERN along the way.

I study computer engineering with a concentration in nanotechnology at Boston University. Most of my time goes to things that have to work the first time: avionics for the Rocket Propulsion Group, firmware for microcontrollers, and the security of systems people trust without thinking about it.

Right now I lead AI safety research on prompt injection in LLM assistants at the BU Security Lab, and help build a multimodal fraud detection system at the Cyber Forensics Lab. Before that I shipped embedded control and network reconnaissance tools at iVORY7 in Piacenza.
